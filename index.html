you may be wondering.

Still have a question? Reach out to the clinic and our team will be happy to help.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment request form on this page or call the clinic. A team member will contact you to confirm an available slot.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?

No. Submitting the form sends an appointment request only. Your appointment is confirmed after the clinic contacts you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a valid ID and any relevant previous prescriptions or test reports you would like your clinician to review. Confirm specific requirements with the clinic.

Can I request a follow-up appointment?

Yes. Choose “Follow-up visit” in the form and add your preferred date and time.

What if I need urgent medical attention?

This website is not an emergency service. For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department.
